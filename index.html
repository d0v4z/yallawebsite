How long does it take?

Most websites go live in under 7 days, once we have your logo, menu or products and photos.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need: a simple website costs less than one with online ordering and an admin panel. Send us a message and we'll give you a clear price, with $200 off if you post about us.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You run the business, we handle the website: domain, hosting, design and updates.

Can I change my menu and prices myself?

Yes, with an admin panel you can change prices, photos and what's sold out from your phone. Or just send us a message and we'll do it.

Will it be in Arabic and English?

Yes. Every site we build has both, with one tap to switch.

Is my customers' data safe?

Your site runs on HTTPS, we only ask customers for what an order needs, and we never collect card details or share data with anyone.

Do I need my own domain?

We set it up for you: yourbusiness.com or another ending you like. Hosting is included.
